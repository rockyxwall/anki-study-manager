Comprehensive syllabus mapping for Anki card taxonomy, chapter tagging, and sequential order.

## Higher Mathematics 1st Paper (উচ্চতর গণিত ১ম পত্র)

| Chapter | Bengali Title | English Title | Core Topics & Key Identifiers | Canonical Tag Prefix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ch 1** | ম্যাট্রিক্স ও নির্ণায়ক | Matrices and Determinants | Transpose, symmetric, skew-symmetric, hermitian, adjoint, inverse matrix, determinant properties, Cramer's rule. | `math::p1::ch1` |
| **Ch 2** | ভেক্টর | Vectors | Vectors in 2D/3D, dot product, cross product, unit vectors, position vector, direction cosines. | `math::p1::ch2` |
| **Ch 3** | সরলরেখা | Straight Lines | Coordinate systems (Cartesian, Polar), distance, internal/external division, area of triangle/polygon, centroid, slopes, straight line equations, perpendicular distance, angle between lines, locus (সঞ্চারপথ). | `math::p1::ch3` |
| **Ch 4** | বৃত্ত | Circles | Circle standard equation, general equation, center $(-g,-f)$, radius $\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}$, circle through 3 points, tangents, normals, chord length (জ্যা), intersection of circle and line, touching circles. | `math::p1::ch4` |
| **Ch 5** | বিন্যাস ও সমাবেশ | Permutations & Combinations | Fundamental counting principle, permutations ($^nP_r$), arrangements, combinations ($^nC_r$), selections. | `math::p1::ch5` |
| **Ch 6** | ত্রিকোণমিতিক অনুপাত | Trigonometric Ratios | Angles in radian/degree, circular measurement ($s = r\theta$), basic trigonometric ratios, quadrant signs, basic reduction ($n\frac{\pi}{2} \pm \theta$). | `math::p1::ch6` |
| **Ch 7** | সংযুক্ত কোণের ত্রিকোণমিতিক অনুপাত | Trigonometric Ratios of Associated/Compound Angles | Compound angles $\sin(A \pm B), \cos(A \pm B), \tan(A \pm B)$, multiple angles ($2A, 3A$), submultiple angles ($A/2$), sum-to-product & product-to-sum transformations, properties of triangles (Sine rule, Cosine rule). | `math::p1::ch7` |
| **Ch 8** | ফাংশন ও ফাংশনের লেখচিত্র | Functions & Graph of Functions | Function definition, domain, range, one-to-one, onto, composite functions, inverse function, graphs of functions. | `math::p1::ch8` |
| **Ch 9** | অন্তরীকরণ | Differentiation | Limits ($\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$), continuity (অবিচ্ছিন্নতা), differentiability, derivatives from first principles, standard derivatives, product/quotient/chain rule, successive differentiation, tangents & normals, maxima & minima. | `math::p1::ch9` |
| **Ch 10** | যোগজীকরণ | Integration | Indefinite integrals, standard forms, integration by substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, definite integrals, area bounded by curves. | `math::p1::ch10` |

## Higher Mathematics 2nd Paper (উচ্চতর গণিত ২য় পত্র)

| Chapter | Bengali Title | English Title | Core Topics & Key Identifiers | Canonical Tag Prefix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ch 1** | বাস্তব সংখ্যা ও অসমতা | Real Numbers and Inequalities | Real number properties, absolute value (পরমমান), linear inequalities in one/two variables, solution sets on number line. | `math::p2::ch1` |
| **Ch 2** | যোগাশ্রয়ী প্রোগ্রাম | Linear Programming | Formulation of LP problems, feasible region, corner point method, maximizing/minimizing objective function. | `math::p2::ch2` |
| **Ch 3** | জটিল সংখ্যা | Complex Numbers | Imaginary unit $i$, powers of $i$, complex number form $z = x + iy$, modulus, argument, polar form, conjugate, cube roots of unity ($1, \omega, \omega^2$), roots of complex numbers, loci in complex plane. | `math::p2::ch3` |
| **Ch 4** | বহুপদী ও বহুপদী সমীকরণ | Polynomials & Polynomial Equations | Polynomials, quadratic equations ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$), discriminant ($b^2 - 4ac$), nature of roots, relations between roots and coefficients, formation of equations, common roots, cubic & quartic equations. | `math::p2::ch4` |
| **Ch 5** | দ্বিপদী বিস্তৃতি | Binomial Expansion | Binomial theorem for positive integral index, general term ($T_{r+1}$), middle term, binomial series for any rational index. | `math::p2::ch5` |
| **Ch 6** | কণিক | Conics | Definition of conics via focus, directrix, eccentricity ($e$). Parabola (পরাবৃত্ত, $e=1$), Ellipse (উপবৃত্ত, $e<1$), Hyperbola (অধিবৃত্ত, $e>1$), standard equations, focal distance, latus rectum, axes. | `math::p2::ch6` |
| **Ch 7** | বিপরীত ত্রিকোণমিতিক ফাংশন ও ত্রিকোণমিতিক সমীকরণ | Inverse Trigonometric Functions & Trigonometric Equations | Principal values, properties of $\sin^{-1}x, \cos^{-1}x, \tan^{-1}x$, identities like $\tan^{-1}x + \tan^{-1}y$, general solutions of $\sin\theta = k, \cos\theta = k, \tan\theta = k$. | `math::p2::ch7` |
| **Ch 8** | স্থিতিবিদ্যা | Statics | Parallelogram law of forces, resultant of concurrent forces, resolution of forces, equilibrium of forces, Lami's theorem (লামির উপপাদ্য), triangle law of forces, parallel forces (like/unlike), moments, couples. | `math::p2::ch8` |
| **Ch 9** | সমতলে বস্তুকণার গতি | Motion of Particles in a Plane (Dynamics) | Motion in a straight line, uniform acceleration formulas ($v = u+at, s = ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$), motion under gravity, vertical projection, projectile motion (প্রাস: range, maximum height, time of flight), relative velocity. | `math::p2::ch9` |
| **Ch 10** | বিস্তার পরিমাপ ও সম্ভাবনা | Measures of Dispersion and Probability | Measures of dispersion (range, mean deviation, standard deviation, variance, coefficient of variation), random experiments, sample space, classical probability, addition and multiplication theorems, conditional probability. | `math::p2::ch10` |

## Foundation / Basics Layer (মৌলিক প্রস্তুতি)
- **Tag Prefix:** `math::basics`
- Scope: High school bridging algebra, radical simplification ($\sqrt{x}$ vs $\pm$), exponent rules, factoring, basic quadratic equation roots before advancing to polynomial equations.

## 3. Canonical Role Taxonomy
- **concept**: Grammar rules, formulas, verb tables, phrase patterns, poem themes, vocabulary meanings (ACTIVE).
- **mcq**: Short gap-filling or multiple-choice test items (ACTIVE).
- **problem**: Full narrative passage conversions, full composition paragraphs (SUSPENDED per Minimum Information Principle).
